How it works

1. Watch real defending

The model studied three full matches of tracking data: where all 22 players and the ball were, 5 times a second.

2. Learn the habit

For each defender it learns one thing: given the ball, the attackers and my team mates, how do I move in the next 0.2 seconds?

3. Let the ghosts play

Five seconds before a shot we swap in the ghosts. The attack plays out exactly as it really did. The ghosts move on their own.

Does the ghost move like a real defender?

Each match was tested with a model that never saw it. The table shows how far off each method is from where the real defenders went, in metres. Lower is better.

What the ghosts found

    From a TV picture to the pitch map

    Tracking data is expensive. So Ghost XI also has code that reads a TV style picture: it finds the camera angle from the white lines, finds the players by shirt colour, and places them on the map.
